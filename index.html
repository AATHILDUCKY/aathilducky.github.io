// about

                  
From curious tinkerer to security engineer

                

                

                  

                    My path into tech started the evening of my last A/L exam in 2020, when I decided to
                    enter IT. What followed was years of relentless self-learning — web development, Python,
                    data science, design, and AI — until I found the field that truly stuck: security.
                  

                  

                    I'm a builder who got into breaking. I explored a lot of disciplines, and what once felt
                    like a lack of focus turned into my biggest advantage: I understand systems from both the
                    developer's and the attacker's side. Every detour eventually led me back to security — so
                    I lean into being versatile while going deep where it matters most.
                  

                  

                    Today I'm a Security Engineer at Welford Systems. I secure backend endpoints and still
                    ship full-stack products and automation tools on the side.
                  

                  

                    Self-taught
